# The 30-hour campaign — 2026-08-22

An operator-granted window spent running simulations rather than reading code.
Two anchor campaigns (a Gate 2U re-measurement and a kit-lane repeatability
sweep), 17 commits, 19 defects.

## THE ONE-LINE FINDING

**Not one defect was in the physics.** Every failure was in the layer that
REPORTS or the layer that SEARCHES, and the recurring shape was a state meaning
*"we cannot answer"* rendering identically to *"here is an answer."*

The unroller is exact on developables (cylinder 0.0000 mm). The KG conversion
was always right in the product path. Viscous drag settles on all 16 archived
hulls. The envelope numbers reproduced identically on an accidental duplicate
run. The machinery held up under every check; the instruments around it did not.

## What the product can and cannot do, measured

| | |
|---|---|
| 6 m / 1.4 t | delivers — 3.09 mm, family (13.5, 6.01, 3.09) PASSES |
| 8 m / 3 t | delivers — 1.47 mm, family (18.4, 1.79, 1.47) PASSES |
| 10 m / 6 t | delivers — 1.92 / 2.31 / 3.88 mm across runs |
| 12 m / 9 t | **the LADDER refuses it**: 0 of 20000 draws are even VALID |
| reliability at 10 m | **2 of 5** at 280 ES steps, reproducible |
| Gate 6D | stays RED — the DEFAULT path is 0 of 7 |

**The 12 m answer is a MISSION problem, not a plywood one**, and saying so
correctly is commercially load-bearing: "your boat cannot be built from
plywood" would be false. This displacement, speed and crew combination has no
compliant hull, and relaxing any of them reopens it.

**Buildability at 10 m is rarer than 1 in 20000.** Zero random draws clear the
bar there and the lane delivers hulls at 1.92 mm. That retires "the search is
flaky": 2 of 5 is the yield of a directed search into a region that sparse, and
it belongs in the specification rather than a bug report.

## Gate 2U: nothing moved

    ran-to-budget   23/25 = 92.0%    archived 15/17 = 88.2%
    SETTLED          5/23 = 21.7%    archived  3/17 = 17.6%
    bar             >= 95%

At the archived N of 17 the re-run reproduced 15/17 and 3-settled EXACTLY. The
layer fixes changed neither rate. 16 of the 18 unsettled cases fail on total
drift, and the total tracks PRESSURE — viscous is 0.00-0.67% in every hull
while pressure is 3.08-21.82%. That is CFD.md section 2's KCS finding on an
unrelated population: **chasing the settled rate means chasing the pressure
integral, not the mesh.**

## Three numbers I reported and had to retract

Each was an instrument measuring partly how the measurement was taken:

| reported | true | cause |
|---|---|---|
| 87% settled | **18.8%** | compared the wrong pair of averaging windows |
| Gate 2U "83.3%" | **17.6%** | the entry's own verify counted a different statistic |
| "2 of 5 at 1500 s" | not reproducible | wall-clock budget: step count tracks machine load |

Operational rule, now in `docs/LESSONS.md`: **before quoting a rate, ask what
would change it besides the thing it is supposed to measure.**

## The mechanism that took four experiments to reject

A basin restart, proposed from plausible reasoning. Rejecting it honestly took:

1. an experiment that measured NOTHING — the trigger needed 637 non-improving
   steps inside a 268-step budget and fired zero times;
2. an experiment that was NOT REPRODUCIBLE — same seed, 2.31 / 2.31 / 8.00 mm;
3. an experiment that was CONFOUNDED — 2 of 5 on wall-clock against 1 of 5 on
   fixed steps, two different experiments, and I was one command from
   reverting on it;
4. three arms, one budget unit, one set of seeds: **control 2/5, capped 1/5,
   uncapped 1/5.** It kills two boats to rescue one.

What survives is worth more than the mechanism: `--refine-steps` (a seeded run
now reproduces), near-miss capture (a refused run keeps its best hull), and a
reliability figure that is finally defensible.

## Single-count scoring is gameable AT ANY COUNT

Scoring the search at n=41 let it harvest the polyline sagitta; two hulls
shipped that the family refused. Moving the score to n=161 killed that exploit
and the search found a new one — a hull reading 38.86 / 39.58 / **2.23** /
40.04 mm at 41 / 81 / 161 / 321. ~40 mm everywhere except the count being
optimised.

The obvious fix is measured and WRONG: scoring the worst of (41, 161) makes
acceptance unreachable, because n=41 reads high for GOOD hulls by design. Every
delivered hull scores 10.9-18.4 mm on it against a 5 mm gate.

**The family verdict is the defence, and it is load-bearing** — the only thing
that stopped a 40 mm hull shipping with a REFOLD VERIFIED stamp.
